Before you order

  • Order the two Saitech parts first (server boards and EPYC processors). They are special order, and the build can't start without them.
  • Newegg pages show an Add to cart button but don't publish stock counts. "Add to cart" below means the listing was orderable when checked, not a guaranteed quantity.
  • Several parts are sold by marketplace sellers on Newegg. Make sure the seller in the cart matches the seller listed here.
  • Prices exclude sales tax. Shipping is included only where the item notes say so.
  • Prices change daily, especially memory and storage. Re-check each link on the day you order.
